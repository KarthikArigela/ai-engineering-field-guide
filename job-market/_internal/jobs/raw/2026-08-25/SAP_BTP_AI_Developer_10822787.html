Job Description
We are seeking a seasoned SAP BTP AI Developer/Consultant to design, develop, and deploy
enterprise-grade Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions on the SAP Business Technology
Platform. The ideal candidate will combine deep technical expertise in BTP extension
frameworks with practical experience in AI orchestration (Joule, RAG) to deliver intelligent
workflows that enhance business processes across S/4HANA landscapes.

Joule AI, SAP integration suite, SAP extension suite, SAP database and data management, SAP
HANA Cloud
