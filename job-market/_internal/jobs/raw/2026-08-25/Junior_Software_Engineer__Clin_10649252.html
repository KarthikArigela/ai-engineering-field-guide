Junior Software Engineer, Clinical AI

The Role
Develop and integrate Clinical AI software components, wrapping models into deployable systems and connecting AI outputs to reporting and viewer applications. Implement front-end modules, PDF report generation, and back-end integration; follow quality/regulatory processes, write documentation, perform code reviews and testing, and support production issues. Collaborate with product and engineering teams throughout the development lifecycle.
Summary Generated by Built In

The Junior Software engineer is primarily responsible for building and maintaining software in the Clinical AI domain. From wrapping deep learning models into containerized medical devices, to integration with other systems that will be used as part of our Clinical Solutions. The focus of the current role is on integration of the results generated by our Clinical AI algorithms with DeepHealth's Reporting and Viewer applications, primarily the Prostate and Lung AI products. This includes front-end modules for our users to interact with the AI results, PDF report generation, but also the backend to ensure smooth integration. 

Essential Duties and Responsibilities   

 

  • Participate in the software development process: Design, refine and develop new features along with maintaining existing ones. You write code and perform code review of you team members, and you contribute to our testing framework by writing and performing automatic and manual tests. To refine requirements, you work with the team lead and the product manager.   

  • Be responsible for the features and projects you work on throughout the entire development cycle. From refinement to seeing it running in production. We stimulate you to understand the workflow from a radiologist’s point of view, which enables you to flag risks and bugs that you identify while developing the software. You will support the service and delivery team if they or our users encounter issues in production.  

  • Maintain and observe all Quality and Regulatory requirements throughout development and release cycle to ensure continued compliance. We work in a regulated quality management system. You will contribute to technical documentation e.g. by writing implementation specifications of the code you write, follow cybersecurity processes, and contribute to risk analysis.  

  • Collaborate with the rest of the team to make strategic recommendations and improvements to the technology and business.  

 

PLEASE NOTE: This is not an exhaustive list of all duties, responsibilities and requirements of the position described above.  Other functions may be assigned and management retains the right to add or change duties at any time.

What We Do

DeepHealth is a wholly-owned subsidiary of RadNet, Inc. (NASDAQ: RDNT) and serves as the umbrella brand for all companies within RadNet’s Digital Health segment. DeepHealth provides AI-powered health informatics with the aim of empowering breakthroughs in care through imaging. Building on the strengths of the companies it has integrated and is rebranding (i.e., eRAD Radiology Information and Image Management Systems and Picture Archiving and Communication System, Aidence lung AI, DeepHealth and Kheiron breast AI and Quantib prostate and brain AI), DeepHealth leverages advanced AI for operational efficiency and improved clinical outcomes in lung, breast, prostate, and brain health. At the heart of DeepHealth’s portfolio is a cloud-native operating system – DeepHealth OS – that unifies data across the clinical and operational workflow and personalizes AI-powered workspaces for everyone in the radiology continuum. Thousands of radiologists at hundreds of imaging centers and radiology departments around the world use DeepHealth solutions to enable earlier, more reliable, and more efficient disease detection, including in large-scale cancer screening programs. DeepHealth’s human-centered, intuitive technology aims to push the boundaries of what’s possible in healthcare.
