Opportunity: Lead Architect & AI-Native Engineer (IC) 
We are looking for an elite Principal Architect who is part visionary, part relentless full
stack executor to join our core team as a high-impact Individual Contributor (IC). 
This is not a role for someone who just wants to draw diagrams or pass off tickets. We 
are looking for a dynamic, high-spirited builder who lives at the bleeding edge of the 
"vibe coding" era—someone who uses AI agents to multiply their output by 10x and 
takes total ownership of the enterprise products they build from the ground up. 

The Role: Architecture Meets Execution 
You will be the tip of the spear for our product feature and module development. You 
will design scalable, distributed solutions and immediately jump into the trenches to 
code them alongside your AI tools. 

Core Mandates: 
• Total Ownership: You own the entire lifecycle of core features—from the initial 
whiteboarding session and architectural design to complex integrations and 
post-launch stability. 
• Enterprise Solutions & Cloud: Architecting and deploying scalable, distributed 
solutions that seamlessly operate across multi-cloud and native environments 
(Azure, AWS, GCP). 
• Agent-Driven Workflows: Architecting complex integrations where autonomous 
AI agents interact with our core APIs, third-party services, and data pipelines to 
drive business logic. 

The Technology Ecosystem: 
You will traverse the entire modern stack, leveraging the right tool for the job. 
• The Full-Stack Core: Deep expertise in TypeScript, Node.js, Next.js, React 19, 
and Tailwind CSS, paired with robust backend prior stack in .NET / C#. 
• Data & Infrastructure: Fluidity across the data spectrum—from PostgreSQL 
and MySQL to in-process and in-memory databases, alongside general ELT and 
Data Engineering proficiency. Deep comfort with Linux and CI/CD pipelines. 
• Security & Ops: Designing secure enterprise integrations utilizing IDP/IAM, 
RBAC, REST/JSON APIs, and robust Observability/Tracing tools. 
• Rapid Delivery: Strategic utilization of low-code/no-code environments to 
accelerate time-to-market without sacrificing enterprise extensibility. 

The "AI-Native" Edge 
• Hyper-Productivity: You are a power user of the new era. Whether it’s Cursor, 
Claude Code, or custom AI assistants, you know how to prompt, guide, and 
debug alongside AI to write highly optimized code at blistering speeds. 
• Agility & Evolution: The tech landscape is shifting daily. You are fiercely curious 
and naturally pivot to adopt the latest paradigms the moment they provide a 
competitive advantage. 

What We’re Looking For: 
• Experience & Impact: 6 to 10+ years of professional experience, though we 
strictly value talent, agility, and output over "years in a seat." 
• Comprehension & Communication: Elite ability to digest complex business 
requirements and articulate architectural trade-offs to both technical peers and 
executive stakeholders. 
• The IC Mentality: You are a self-starter who doesn't need a team to execute a 
vision. You are both the architect and the engine. 
• Passion: You are high-energy, naturally curious, and genuinely excited about the 
current paradigm shift in software development. 

Why Join? 
You will have the autonomy to dictate architecture, bypass corporate red tape, and build 
at a velocity most teams only dream of. This is a chance to work in a lean, high-trust 
environment where you get a bird’s-eye view of modern enterprise architecture while 
keeping your hands deep in the most exciting tech available today. 

Does this sound like how you already work? Reach out directly with a brief overview of 
the most complex module or architecture you’ve shipped recently and tell me how you 
used AI agents to get it over the finish line. Let's build something incredible. 

Skills Required

  • 6 to 10+ years of professional software engineering experience
  • Expertise in TypeScript, Node.js, Next.js, and React
  • Experience with Tailwind CSS
  • Backend experience with .NET and C#
  • Experience with PostgreSQL and MySQL
  • Familiarity with in-process and in-memory databases and ELT/data engineering
  • Strong Linux and CI/CD pipeline experience
  • Cloud experience (Azure, AWS, GCP)
  • Experience designing secure integrations using IDP/IAM, RBAC, REST/JSON APIs
  • Experience with observability and tracing tools for production systems
  • Hands-on experience using AI agents, prompting, and tools like Cursor or Claude Code
  • Ability to own full feature lifecycle and communicate architectural trade-offs to stakeholders
  • Self-starter individual contributor mentality (IC) capable of independent delivery
  • Familiarity with low-code/no-code platforms to accelerate delivery
