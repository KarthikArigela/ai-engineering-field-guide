The Role
The Applied AI Engineer will implement AI tools, enhance workflows, and provide in-house consulting to optimize team usage of AI at Upvest, adhering to compliance and business outcomes.
Summary Generated by Built In

At Upvest, we are on a mission to make investing as easy as spending money. Upvest empowers businesses to offer a wide range of investment products and the best experience in the field of capital market investment and retirement planning. Upvest’s Investment API is easy to integrate so that fintechs and financial institutions can save resources and fully focus on their core business.

We are proud to partner with Europe’s leading Fintechs and financial institutions such as DKB, Revolut, N26 and Raisin. Founded in 2017 by Martin Kassing, Upvest now brings together over 270 talented professionals from more than 70 nationalities. Upvest is backed by €280M in total funding from world-class investors, including BlackRock, Tencent, Sapphire Ventures, and Bessemer Venture Partners, Earlybird, Notion Capital, and Motive. Our latest €105M funding round in March 2026 - led by Sapphire and Tencent - serves as a massive catalyst for our growth, allowing us to offer premier investment experience.


About the Role

As we enter our next stage of growth, we are looking for an exceptional AI Platform Engineer to join our Engineering team, reporting directly to Yurii Opryshko and working closely together with our SVP Engineering Roberto Fajardo.This is a senior individual contributor role, and one of the first two hires into a new Applied AI function.
AI is reshaping how financial infrastructure companies operate — and at Upvest, we're moving fast. Our engineers have taken to it quickly. The rest of the company hasn't had the same run, and that's the gap this role exists to close.

Product engineering stays focused on the core product. Your job is the layer underneath everything else: the shared infrastructure, tooling and standards that let every team at Upvest build with AI safely — and, crucially, the work that makes those teams able to do it without you.

You will be one of two people in this function. Your counterpart comes from the business side and will spend their time in Operations, Growth, Compliance and Finance finding the problems worth solving. You'll be the one who makes solving them possible, repeatable, and safe.

Build and run the AI platform

You will design, build and operate the infrastructure that connects Claude and other LLMs to Upvest's internal systems — MCP servers, integrations to internal tools, APIs and data sources — and the hosted infrastructure behind the automation stack that other teams build on.

You don't build in isolation. You plug into what our platform and engineering teams already run rather than standing up a parallel stack, and you're as willing to adopt and extend something that exists as you are to build it yourself.

As adoption grows, you harden what you've built. Reliability, observability and evals for AI systems in production are your responsibility, not an afterthought.


Make other teams self-sufficient

This is half the job, not a footnote.

You will work directly with people in Operations, Compliance, Growth and Finance to help them build their own workflows — sitting with them, understanding the actual process, and shipping the first version together. Then you make the second one something they can do without you: templates, patterns, guardrails, and enough teaching that the knowledge stays with the team rather than with you.

The measure of success is not how many automations you've built. It's how many happen without you. If ten workflows are live and all ten depend on you personally, the platform hasn't worked.

You seek out the AI champions already experimenting across the company and turn what they're doing into something secure, compliant and durable — without taking it away from them.


Work alongside Security, not around them

Upvest is a BaFin and FCA regulated firm. You understand the constraints that come with that and don't need convincing they're real.

Security owns the controls. You are the main technical point of contact between them and everything the AI platform touches — translating how AI systems reach data and tools into terms Security can assess, and building so that access boundaries, audit trails and data handling are there by design rather than retrofitted.


What you bring

  • Platform mindset, applied pragmatically: You've built infrastructure others built on top of. You also know when not to. You'd rather ship a narrow slice that creates value this week and generalise it once you've seen the same problem three times than design the general case before you understand it.

  • You make other people capable: You've handed something over and had it stick. You can point to work where the team you built for ended up able to carry it themselves, and you can say what made that work.

  • AI native: Hands-on experience shipping AI agents and end-to-end automations that ran in production and that other people depended on — not prototypes. You move fast and stay current.

  • Connecting LLMs to internal systems: You've securely connected models to internal tools, APIs and data sources, and you understand the access, scoping and audit questions that come with it. Experience building or operating MCP servers is a strong plus.

  • Automation and orchestration infrastructure: You've built and run workflow automation at production quality. We use n8n; the specific tool matters less than having done this for real.

  • Security-conscious collaborator: Comfortable being the technical counterpart to a Security team on AI systems — access control, data boundaries, audit trails — without owning security decisions yourself.

  • Regulated industry experience: You've worked in fintech or a closely adjacent field and understand compliance constraints first-hand.

  • Pragmatism: You define ROI, iterate and ship. A simple deterministic automation beats a complex agent when appropriate, and you can tell the difference.

  • Background: 5+ years in a scale-up, in roles such as platform engineer, infrastructure engineer, applied AI engineer, solutions engineer, or similar.

  • Location: Berlin preferred. English required; German a plus.

This is not a data science role. We are looking for someone who applies and deploys AI, not someone who trains models or builds ML pipelines.

Where this goes

You're joining Engineering Enablement, an established team of eight engineers, so you won't be starting from zero — there's existing infrastructure, existing relationships across the business, and groundwork already underway. As the function proves itself and grows, it becomes its own team.

How we Upvest in you

  • Best-in-class AI tools: Every Upvenger has €20,000 per year to spend on the best AI tools available — so you're always working with the most powerful models and tooling on the market.

  • Work with experts: Join a mission-driven engineering team solving hard, real-world problems at the intersection of AI and regulated financial infrastructure.

  • Professional development: Access an annual budget of €1,500 for personal and professional training to master your craft.

  • Wellbeing: Recharge with 30 days of annual leave and maintain a healthy lifestyle with sports benefits. Access confidential professional coaching and enjoy the flexibility to work remotely abroad for up to 183 days a year.

  • Compensation and equity: We believe that all Upvengers contribute to our success and deserve a competitive, above-market salary and a participation in our employee equity program.

  • Flexible work environment: This role is based in Berlin. We are committed to flexibility — we understand you don't always have to be in the office to do your best work.

  • Team celebrations: Participate in company-wide events such as UpFest, dinners, offsites and our holiday party to connect with colleagues and celebrate our achievements.

Our Values

  • Make it easy for others. We simplify the complex and act with the best intentions.

  • Own the outcome. We are proactive, fast and confident to get the job done, valuing progress over perfection.

  • Rise to the challenge. We aim high and push the boundaries. We stay curious, learn and celebrate our wins together.

  • Tell the story. We start with the Why to align on purpose. We are transparent and share knowledge to empower and inspire others.

Upvest is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.

Skills Required

  • 5+ years in a scale-up roles such as solutions engineer or AI product engineer
  • Hands-on experience shipping AI agents and building automations with tools like n8n
  • Experience with MCP servers and connecting LLMs to internal systems
  • Experience in fintech or a closely adjacent field
  • Ability to coach non-technical teams on AI tooling
