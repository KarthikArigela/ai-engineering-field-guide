Lead AI/ML Engineer

Noida, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, IND

The Role
Design, implement, optimize, and deploy ML and NLP models into production. Perform data preprocessing, experiments, analysis, and reporting. Maintain model documentation, apply MLOps/CI-CD, and communicate findings to stakeholders while staying current on AI research and ethics.
Summary Generated by Built In
3Pillar is an AI transformation partner on a mission to help enterprises build the AI-native products and intelligent agents that will define the next era of business. With teams across North America, Europe, Latin America, and Asia, we work with the most ambitious companies in financial services, healthcare, media, and technology — helping them move faster, modernize boldly, and compete on their own terms. Our HelixAI platform and Helix Pods delivery model put our engineers at the center of real agentic transformation — doing work that is open, portable, and built to last. We are building the future of enterprise AI

Key Responsibilities:

    • Understanding the business requirements

    • Implement and deploy ML algorithms and models into production environments. 

    • Optimize and fine-tune AI/ML models to improve accuracy and efficiency, and maintain the documentation.

    • Write clean, scalable, and optimized code for ML models and APIs.

    • Conduct experiments, perform data analysis, and present findings to stakeholders.

    • Stay up-to-date with the latest AI/ML research and technologies and apply them to improve our platform.

Minimum Qualifications:

    • Bachelors or Masters degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related fields.

    • 2-4 years of experience in working with Python, training / optimizing ML models , NLP and deep learning.

    • Knowledge of Neural Networks; CNNs, RNNs, VAE.

    • Experience with TensorFlow / Keras, PyTorch, Jupyter Notebooks / Anaconda

    • Knowledge of Matplotlib, Seaborn, or Plotly

    • Experience with any cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.

    • Solid understanding of machine learning concepts, algorithms, and statistical methods. 

    • Experience with data preprocessing, cleaning, and feature engineering techniques.

    • Strong analytical, problem-solving skills, attention to detail, ability to work independently and with a team.

    • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to present complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.

    • Understanding of data privacy, AI Ethics and model governance

    • Experience with computer vision libraries (OpenCV, etc.) and NLP libraries (spaCy, Hugging Face, etc.). 

    • Knowledge of MLOps practices and experience with CI/CD tools.

      • Additional Experience Desired:

      • Understanding of Software Engineering frameworks and methodologies.

      • Good to have knowledge of NLP techniques and tools, such as SpaCy, NLTK.

      • Working experience with Generative AI problems will be an added advantage

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or related field.

  • Knowledge of neural network architectures (CNNs, RNNs, VAE).
  • Experience with TensorFlow or Keras.

  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
  • Solid understanding of machine learning concepts, algorithms, and statistical methods.
  • Experience with data preprocessing, cleaning, and feature engineering techniques.
  • Experience with computer vision libraries (OpenCV) and NLP libraries (spaCy, Hugging Face).
  • Knowledge of MLOps practices and experience with CI/CD tools.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; ability to work independently and in a team.
  • Excellent communication skills; able to present technical concepts to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Understanding of data privacy, AI ethics, and model governance.
  • Understanding of software engineering frameworks and methodologies.
  • Familiarity with additional NLP tools such as NLTK.
  • Working experience with generative AI problems.
