Company Description

NIQ is the world's leading consumer intelligence company, with the most complete view of consumer buying behavior across more than 90 countries. We are building a new kind of team inside NIQ: small forward-deployed teams that embed with the world's largest consumer and retail enterprises and build AI-powered decision applications on NIQ data joined with the client's own — working software in weeks, measured on the client's business results.

Job Description

About the role

The Forward Deployed AI Engineer is the builder on the team. You operate like an early-stage startup CTO inside a Fortune 500 client: deep, fast, and comfortable with ambiguity. You write production-grade code, ship a working v0 in weeks — not quarters — and measure your own success by movement in the client's numbers, not code shipped.

What you'll do

  • Build data pipelines joining NIQ's consumer and retail data with the client's own sales, inventory, and margin data
  • Design and ship AI decision applications: recommendation workflows, LLM-agent-driven analysis, approval flows with guardrails, full auditability
  • Navigate broken, incomplete enterprise data environments — and ship anyway
  • Work in a 2–3 person deployment team with a data scientist and an AI Deployment Architect, dedicated to one client
  • Surface reusable patterns from each deployment back into NIQ's product platform

Qualifications

What we're looking for

• Strong software engineering fundamentals: production Python and SQL, data pipelines, APIs

• Hands-on with modern AI tooling — LLM APIs, agent frameworks, retrieval systems

• Full-stack enough to ship a usable application end-to-end on your own

• Bias to speed and client impact over perfect architecture

• Comfortable client-facing: you demo your own work and defend your own trade-offs

Nice to have

• Forward-deployed, solutions engineering, or startup experience

• Experience with retail, CPG, or commercial data at scale

Additional Information

Why this role

You will be on the founding team of a new motion inside a global company — with the autonomy of a startup and the data assets of a market leader. Success is measured on client outcomes, not deliverables, and this role is a direct path to leadership as the deployment portfolio grows

Other benefits include flexible working environment, comprehensive health insurance, industry-leading parental leave, life insurance, education support and more
