The Role
The Staff Software Engineer will lead architecture and development of scalable enterprise platforms, managing full engineering lifecycle and mentoring engineers, while driving modernization initiatives in cloud infrastructure and CI/CD systems.
Summary Generated by Built In

We're looking for a Staff Engineer who can lead both product engineering and cloud infrastructure across a mature .NET platform running on AWS. Your immediate focus will be helping modernize and scale a business-critical application using C#, .NET, React, TypeScript, AWS, and CI/CD automation.

What's particularly interesting about this opportunity is where the platform is headed next.

As part of the long-term technology strategy, you'll play a key role in evaluating and driving the evolution of the platform toward a more modern architecture centered around React, Node.js, AWS Cloud Services, AI-powered capabilities, and cloud-native engineering practices. This means you'll not only help solve today's engineering challenges but also influence the future technical direction of the product.

For engineers who enjoy owning architecture, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, and product delivery end-to-end—and who are excited by the opportunity to work with the technologies shaping the next generation of software platforms—this role offers the chance to have a significant impact at the Staff Engineer level.

 

What You’ll Do
  • Lead architecture and development across enterprise .NET applications using C#, ASP.NET MVC, and modern backend engineering practices
  • Design and maintain AWS cloud infrastructure using AWS CDK in TypeScript across multi-environment deployments
  • Build and optimize CI/CD pipelines using AWS CodePipeline and CodeBuild, including Windows-based build agents for .NET workloads
  • Own infrastructure provisioning, deployment orchestration, release automation, and environment management strategies
  • Develop and maintain React and TypeScript frontend applications integrated with .NET backend services
  • Drive modernization initiatives including clean architecture adoption, refactoring, and technical debt reduction
  • Design scalable PostgreSQL and MSSQL database solutions with strong focus on schema design, query optimization, and performance tuning
  • Troubleshoot complex infrastructure, deployment, and platform issues across application and cloud environments
  • Champion DevOps best practices including automation, operational excellence, monitoring, governance, and deployment reliability
  • Monitor platform health, cloud costs, observability, and incident response processes across AWS environments
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to improve developer workflows, reduce operational toil, and increase engineering efficiency
  • Mentor engineers on cloud engineering, CI/CD, AWS best practices, software design, and scalable system architecture
  • Contribute to future platform evolution initiatives including MERN stack modernization planning and cloud-native architecture adoption
What You’ll BringRequired Qualifications
  • 10+ years of experience delivering scalable enterprise software solutions while owning both application development and cloud infrastructure
  • Strong expertise in C# and .NET technologies including ASP.NET MVC, Web Forms, and enterprise backend architecture
  • Proven experience leading technical initiatives, mentoring engineers, and influencing engineering standards across teams
  • Strong understanding of modern software design principles including SOLID principles, clean architecture, and scalable engineering practices
  • Hands-on experience designing and operating AWS cloud infrastructure in production environments
  • Experience building secure, scalable CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation systems
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent professional experience
Hands-On Technical ExpertiseApplication Development
  • C#, .NET, ASP.NET MVC
  • React and TypeScript frontend development
  • PostgreSQL and MSSQL database design and optimization
  • REST APIs and enterprise application architecture
AWS Cloud & Infrastructure
  • AWS CDK in TypeScript
  • Lambda, EC2, ECS, S3, IAM, VPC
  • CloudWatch, Secrets Manager, Systems Manager
  • CodePipeline, CodeBuild, CloudFormation
  • Infrastructure as Code and deployment automation
DevOps & Platform Engineering
  • CI/CD pipeline architecture and release engineering
  • Windows build agents for .NET applications
  • Monitoring, observability, and operational excellence
  • Scalability, security, governance, and cloud reliability best practices
Nice to Have (We’ll Support You If You Don’t Yet)
  • Docker and ECS for containerized workloads
  • GitHub Actions or additional CI/CD tooling
  • Multi-account AWS environments and AWS Organizations
  • Node.js ecosystem experience relevant to MERN modernization initiatives
  • Monitoring and observability platforms such as Datadog or Grafana
  • Enterprise security and compliance standards including SOC 2, ISO, or PCI
  • AWS cost optimization and FinOps practices
  • Exposure to AI/ML integrations including AWS Bedrock, MCP servers, or RAG-based systems

About Us

Diligent is the AI leader in governance, risk and compliance (GRC) SaaS solutions, helping more than 1 million users and 700,000 board members to clarify risk and elevate governance. The Diligent One Platform gives practitioners, the C-Suite and the board a consolidated view of their entire GRC practice so they can more effectively manage risk, build greater resilience and make better decisions, faster. 

At Diligent, we're building the future with people who think boldly and move fast.  Whether you're designing systems that leverage large language models or part of a team reimaging workflows with AI, you'll help us unlock entirely new ways of working and thinking.  Curiosity is in our DNA, we look for individuals willing to ask the big questions and experiment fearlessly - those who embrace change not as a challenge, but as an opportunity.  The future belongs to those who keep learning, and we are building it together.  At Diligent, you’re not just building the future - you’re an agent of positive change, joining a global community on a mission to make an impact.

Learn more at diligent.com or follow us on LinkedIn and Facebook

What We Do

Diligent is the leading GRC SaaS provider, serving nearly 1 million users from 25,000 organizations around the world. Our software enables holistic and informed conversations about governance, risk and compliance and ensures CEOs, CFOs and the board have an integrated view of audit, risk, information security, ethics and compliance from across the organization. Our world-changing idea is to bring technology, insights and confidence to leaders so they can build more effective, equitable, and successful organizations – and create lasting, positive impact on the world. We seek to empower organizations to be better for their stakeholders and communities, for their customers and employees, for their bottom line.
