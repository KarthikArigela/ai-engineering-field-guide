About Gusto

At Gusto, we're on a mission to grow the small business economy. We handle the hard stuff — payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and HR — so owners can focus on their craft and their customers. With teams in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, we support more than 500,000 small businesses nationwide and are building a workplace that reflects the people we serve.

 
All full-time employees receive competitive base pay, benefits, and equity (RSUs) — because everyone who helps build Gusto should share in its success. Offer amounts are determined by role, level, and location. Learn more about our Total Rewards philosophy.

 
AI is a fundamental part of how work gets done at Gusto. We expect all team members to actively engage with AI tools relevant to their role and grow their fluency as the technology evolves. AI experience requirements vary by role and will be assessed during the interview process.

Location Preference for this role are: San Francisco Bay Area, New York City Metro, or Seattle Metro

About the Role

We're hiring an experienced full stack engineer to help small businesses work better with AI. You'll build Gus, Gusto's AI-powered assistant, and create scalable solutions that serve millions of users. As a Software Engineer at this level, you'll own projects end-to-end from initial specs to deployed features—working at the intersection of modern AI capabilities and real business needs.

About the Team

The Core Experience - AI Experiences team owns Gus, Gusto's AI-powered assistant, and the platform that enables teams across Gusto to deliver AI-native experiences to customers across web, mobile, voice, email, and agent-based channels. You'll work within Gusto's AI platform infrastructure, collaborating closely with sister teams who own the underlying agent framework. Your focus is building experiences and enabling product teams to extend and scale within the platform.

This work is directly tied to Gusto's top priorities. We operate with fast iteration cycles, deep cross-functional collaboration, and high ownership.

Here's what you'll do day-to-day:
  • Architect, build, and test technologies supporting AI experiences within Gusto's product suite
  • Create standardized, self-serve patterns that enable product teams to extend Gus across web, voice, email, and agent-based experiences
  • Collaborate with ML and product teams on prompt engineering, clarifying question flows, and escalation logic—using A/B tests and data to drive improvements
  • Mentor engineers on complex technical challenges in AI, scalability, and platform infrastructure
  • Help scale a large Ruby/Rails and React application serving millions of users
  • Work with Product, Design, Security, and Technical Operations teams to understand customer needs, develop solutions, and launch features
  • Build customer-facing products end-to-end that customers rely on every day
Here's what we're looking for:
  • 8+ years of software development experience, with 4+ years building scalable platforms or high-impact customer-facing products
  • Strong foundation in front-end engineering and modern web technologies (Ruby on Rails, React, GraphQL, or similar full-stack frameworks) with ability to build well-structured, maintainable code
  • Track record of owning multi-quarter projects that solve complex engineering problems and deliver measurable customer impact at scale
  • Experience building internal platforms or developer-facing infrastructure that enables other teams to self-serve and ship AI-powered features
  • Excellent cross-functional communication skills with ability to mentor engineers and collaborate effectively across teams and stakeholders
  • Product-minded, customer-facing engineering approach with passion for creating great customer experiences and ownership over performance, reliability, and quality
  • Bonus if you have AI platform development experience or strong learning orientation toward AI technologies, patterns, and frameworks (orchestration, evals, prompt systems—not ML model development)

Our cash compensation amount for this role is targeted at $163,000-$204,000 in Denver, $178,000-$223,000 for Los Angeles and most remote locations, $197,000-$247,000 for San Francisco, New York, and Seattle. Final offer amounts are determined by multiple factors including candidate experience, level, and expertise and may vary from the amounts listed above.

Gusto has physical office spaces in Denver, San Francisco, and New York City. Employees who are based in those locations will be expected to work from the office on designated days approximately 2-3 days per week (or more depending on role). The same office expectations apply to all Symmetry roles, Gusto's subsidiary, whose physical office is in Scottsdale.

Note: The San Francisco office expectations encompass both the San Francisco and San Jose metro areas. 

When approved to work from a location other than a Gusto office, a secure, reliable, and consistent internet connection is required. This includes non-office days for hybrid employees.

What We Do

Gusto is a modern, online small business platform that helps small businesses take care of their teams. On top of full-service payroll, Gusto offers health insurance, 401(k)s, expert HR, and team management tools. Today, Gusto offices in Denver, San Francisco, and New York serve more than 400,000 businesses. Our customers come from all walks of life, and so do we. We hire great people from a wide variety of backgrounds, not just because it's the right thing to do, but because it makes our company stronger. If you share our values and our enthusiasm for small businesses, you will find a home at Gusto.

Why Work With Us

We help small businesses—the backbone of the economy—thrive. Everything we do ladders up to empowering entrepreneurs and supporting their teams. Gusto empowers builders at every level to take initiative and make things better. You'll be solving meaningful challenges that shape the future of work.
