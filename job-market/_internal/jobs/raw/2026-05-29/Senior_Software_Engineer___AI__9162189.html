Senior Software Engineer – AI and .Net

WHAT MAKES US, US  

Join some of the most innovative thinkers in FinTech as we lead the evolution of financial technology. If you are an innovative, curious, collaborative person who embraces challenges and wants to grow, learn and pursue outcomes with our prestigious financial clients, say Hello to SimCorp!  

At its foundation, SimCorp is guided by our values — caring, customer success-driven, collaborative, curious, and courageous. Our people-centered organization focuses on skills development, relationship building, and client success. We take pride in cultivating an environment where all team members can grow, feel heard, valued, and empowered. 

If you like what we’re saying, keep reading!  

WHY THIS ROLE IS IMPORTANT TO US

In SimCorp, we help some of the biggest financial institutions in the world be successful by simplifying their accounting, operations, and investments. We do this through IT systems, processes, and financial knowledge. High quality, performing, secure, and cost-effective software is at the core of what we do. To build, maintain and operate this software, engineers are essential to us. 
 
You will be working with a team of about 12 people located in Noida/Hyderabad in India and Copenhagen, Denmark. The Product Development team functions as a center of enablement for artificial intelligence (AI), providing knowledge and guidelines to other teams, as well as working hands on implementing and operating solutions with AI. 

WHAT YOU WILL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

The Senior Software Engineer in AI Team at SimCorp, at the IC4 grade, is responsible for the design, development, and implementation of complex software solutions. This role involves collaborating with cross-functional teams to develop high-quality software that meets business requirements and technical specifications. The Senior Software Engineer takes ownership of key engineering projects, provides technical guidance to junior engineers, and ensures that software is delivered on time and within scope. This position requires strong problem-solving skills, expertise in software engineering practices, and the ability to work in a collaborative and fast-paced environment.

We work team-based, so your primary responsibility is as a team member. You need to make sure the team delivers on its commitments, that it’s a good place to work for you and your teammates, and that it continuously improves. 

Daily tasks are for example: 

  • Developing plugins and tools for generative AI models, Agentic AI solutions. 

  • Going from proof-of-concepts to high quality, production ready solutions. 

  • Integrating AI functionality with modules and Agents developed by other teams 

  • Collaborate with product management, design, and quality assurance teams to ensure seamless integration and delivery of software solutions 

  • Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues, ensuring high-quality solutions that are scalable, reliable, and performant 

  • Ensure adherence to software development best practices, including coding standards, testing, and documentation 

  • Continuously assess and improve engineering processes, recommending new tools and technologies to enhance productivity and software quality 

  • Participate in code reviews, ensuring that best practices are followed and maintains high standards for software quality 

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Most importantly, you can see yourself contributing and thriving in the position described above. How you gained the skills needed for doing that is less important. We expect you to be good at several of the following and be able to - and interested in - learning the rest.

Most importantly, you can see yourself contributing and thriving in the position described above. 

  • Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field. 

  • 4+ years of practical experience with .NET technologies (C#, Microsoft .NET)

  • Experience in building AI solutions (1+ years), with demonstrated interest in Agentic AI and associated projects will be an added advantage

  • Experience with generative AI tools like OpenAI for Azure, protocols like A2A/MCP plugins & function calls for example through SemanticKernel. 

  • Comprehensive understanding and practical experience with application architecture, covering both frontend and backend systems. 

  • Knowledge in working with Azure and Azure DevOps. 

  • Experience with Agile development methodologies, as well as DevOps and CI/CD practices 

As a person, you have the following traits: 

  • Accepting responsibility and taking initiative

  • Earn trust, think big, and take calculated risks. 

  • You collaborate with team members to work towards common goals. 

  • Solid problem-solving skills and comfortable questioning assumptions and as-is. 

  • Exhibits a consistent approach and a creative mindset. 

  • Thrives in a collaborative environment and values teamwork 

  • Like to have fun at work and positivity comes easily to you 

  • Advanced English verbal and communication skills

BENEFITS

  • Global hybrid work policy - We ask you to work 2 days a week from the office. If you choose you can work remotely the other days. Of course you are welcome at the office if that is your preference.

  • Growth and innovation - Every 6th sprint is reserved for planning and innovation. So, with regular intervals you have a chance to explore and learn new skills or improve something that you believe will be beneficial to you, your team or the application.

  • Self-Direction - High degree of self-organization. Each team and developer have a high degree of freedom to plan, organize and design their work.

  • Inclusive and diverse company culture

  • Work-life balance – We believe that an equilibrium between professional responsibilities makes us all the best version of ourselves, both in private life and as colleagues in the workplace

  • Empowerment – We believe that all voices are valuable and must be heard. You will be involved in shaping our work processes
