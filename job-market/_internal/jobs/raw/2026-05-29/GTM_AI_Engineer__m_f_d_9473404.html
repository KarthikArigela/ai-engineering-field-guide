GTM AI Engineer (m/f/d)

This newly created role will join Moonfare’s AI & Transformation team as part of the GTM Engineering function. You will be responsible for building and scaling Moonfare’s commercial systems and processes. The team sits at the intersection of Investor Relations (Sales), Account Management, Marketing, and Product, owning the infrastructure, data, and automation that power revenue generation. As we’re getting started, we will automate 1-2 processes per quarter. This team is responsible for the CRM system, commercial processes, and performance monitoring.

You will turn manual processes into automated and agentic workflows across lead generation, scoring, routing, and outreach, directly impacting revenue and efficiency. The challenge is making AI work in a complex regulated system with a long sales cycle and not just in theory. This is a high-impact role shaping how AI is applied in production across our commercial teams.


Your Mission:

  • Build and maintain automation in HubSpot (workflows, lead routing, lifecycle management)
  • Define and own the architecture of Moonfare's AI-driven GTM engine, setting the standards for how automation and AI are built, evaluated, and scaled across commercial teams
  • Design and implement lead scoring models (rules-based + AI-assisted)
  • Manage HubSpot connectors to Claude and other tools
  • Improve data, lead generation, and enrichment processes (within compliant sources)
  • Turn manual GTM processes and turn them into scalable agentic processes
  • Continuously build and implement AI-driven automation use cases across GTM engineering and commercial teams, from idea to production

The next Moonfarian:

  • You bring an extensive track record working with HubSpot
  • Strong understanding of CRM architecture and data models
  • Experience with complex workflows and automation
  • Hands-on use of LLMs (e.g. Claude) in production workflows via tools like n8n, APIs, or similar automation frameworks
  • Passion to coach others on AI and multiply the knowledge
  • Experience applying AI to lead scoring, prioritization, personalization, data classification, enrichment
  • Familiarity with APIs and integrations
  • Ability to integrate AI into systems
  •  

If you feel you do not meet all of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply!

Please Note: We can only accept applications from those eligible to live and work in Berlin. This is a hybrid role with office attendance required 4 days/week. We do not accept applications sent by email. You should submit your application directly through our careers website.

  • Personal Growth: We support your development with an individual growth budget of €1,500 or 2% of your base salary (whichever is higher).
  • Invest in Your Future: We support your long-term financial goals with a company pension scheme, providing a 20% employer contribution.
  • Relax and Recharge: You start with 25 vacation days and you’ll receive one additional day for each year of tenure, up to a maximum of 30 days.
  • Daily Lunch: Enjoy a free lunch every day that you are in the Berlin office, with a weekly changing menu that includes salads, curries, pastas, and a variety of vegan and vegetarian options.
  • Subsidised Public Transport: We support your commute with a monthly subsidy for the Deutschland job ticket, making your travel easier and more affordable.
  • Fitness and Wellbeing: Stay active with a subsidised Urban Sports Club membership, giving you flexible access to a wide variety of sports and wellness activities across Germany.
  • A Culture of Excellence: Join a team of ambitious, smart, and committed professionals who are passionate about revolutionising private market investing.
