Who We Are

Notion is the collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together. We're building one place where your knowledge, projects, meetings, and AI tools live side by side, so work feels faster, clearer, and less fragmented. Millions of individuals, small teams, and large companies run their work on Notion.

Notinos (our employees) are customer zero in bringing this future of work to life. We care about craft, humanity, and building things that last — not just shipping the next feature, but setting a standard for how modern teams (with humans and agents working together) think and execute.

About the Role

Notion is building the operating system for knowledge work. We aspire for Notion users to be able to do substantive knowledge work from their phone.

AI is what makes that possible in Notion by bringing the full power of the workspace into your pocket — understanding context, taking action, and helping you move work forward in the moment. On mobile, that’s not just about speed and polish but also about expanding what users can do with AI: capture and structure information, answer questions across their workspace, manage tasks and projects, coordinate with teammates, and trigger or automate workflows — all with trustworthy context, clear collaboration cues, and dependable execution.

What You'll Achieve:

  • Build and ship AI-first iOS experiences from interaction design through implementation, launch, and iteration.

  • Raise the bar on reliability and performance improving crash prevention, fast time-to-first-token, efficient networking, and memory or battery discipline.

  • Evolve our architecture and tooling through modularization, observability, offline or resilience strategies, and test coverage.

  • Collaborate deeply with Product, Design, Data Science, and Research to turn ambiguous problems into crisp shipped outcomes.

  • Work across the stack when needed to deliver cohesive AI workflows.

Skills You'll Need to Bring

  • Ability to ship quality apps at scale: You have around 8-10+ years of experience delivering high-impact iOS features for large audiences, and you’re fluent in Swift and core iOS frameworks.

  • Thoughtful problem-solving: You start by building a clear model of the problem, then iterate toward a clean solution — independently or with teammates — and you ask for help early when you’re stuck.

  • Rapid iteration and learning: You build with rigor and momentum, stay open to trying things, and use rapid prototyping to learn fast with an optimistic, solutions-first mindset.

  • AI product instincts: You tinker with and explore the latest AI tools and can design AI UX people can understand and trust (streaming UX, tool-use patterns, guardrails, and failure modes).

  • Empathetic communication: You communicate nuanced ideas clearly in writing and in person, and you navigate disagreements with curiosity and respect.

Nice to Haves

  • You’ve built and shipped AI-powered mobile experiences (voice, multimodal input, tool use, evaluation or quality loops).

  • 0→1 product experience, or experience rapidly iterating on new product surfaces with tight feedback loops.

  • Experience with realtime collaboration, offline-first sync, or rich-text editing.

  • You’re proficient with our broader stack (TypeScript, React, Node), or you’re excited to learn it to deliver end-to-end mobile experiences.

A Note on AI

You don’t need deep AI expertise for every role, but we do expect every Notino to be intellectually curious, drawn to tinkering and discovery, and excited to use AI as a real collaborator in their work. For some roles, AI fluency is a core requirement — when that’s the case, we’ll make it explicit in the qualifications. People who thrive here don’t treat AI as a novelty. They use it to think better, move faster, and build more creatively.

What We Do

Notion blends your everyday work tools into one. Product roadmap? Company wiki? Meeting notes? With Notion, they're all in one place, and totally customizable to meet the needs of any workflow. It's the all-in-one workspace for you, your team, and your whole company. Mission: We humans are toolmakers by nature, but most of us can't build or modify the software we use every day — arguably our most powerful tool. Here at Notion, we're on a mission to make it possible for everyone to shape the tools that shape their lives.

Why Work With Us

Here at Notion, our work shapes our culture and our culture inspires our work. We seek to hire creative toolmakers that want to be the best in their craft. If every employee is able to focus on being the best toolmaker in their craft, we'll be able to achieve our mission of enabling the world to better solve its problems.
