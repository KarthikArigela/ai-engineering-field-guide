The Role

Nansen is a leading blockchain analytics platform that empowers investors and professionals with real-time, actionable insights derived from on-chain data.


The Opportunity:

Nansen is leveling up its strategic capabilities in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). We are actively seeking a highly skilled and motivated Senior AI/ML Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will develop and monitor AI models to analyze vast amounts of blockchain data, identifying patterns and insights that drive decision-making for crypto investors. Your contributions will be key to advancing Nansen's mission of surfacing the signal and creating winners in the future of finance.

As a Senior AI/ML Engineer at Nansen, you will play a crucial part in enhancing our platform's capabilities, enabling us to offer a cutting-edge product to our users. Your expertise will not only influence the direction of our technology but also solidify Nansen's position as a leader in blockchain analytics.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead the design, implementation, and use of AI/ML models to process and analyze blockchain data, ensuring high performance and scalability.
  • Collaborate with product managers and engineers to integrate AI/ML models into Nansen's platform, enhancing its analytical capabilities and improving user experience.
  • Stay abreast of the latest developments in AI/ML and blockchain technologies, applying innovative approaches to solve complex challenges.
  • Mentor junior engineers and promote best practices in AI/ML development across the team.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to align AI/ML initiatives with business objectives and customer needs.

Requirements:

  • Proven experience as an AI/ML Engineer or similar role, with a strong portfolio of successful AI/ML projects.
  • Expertise in machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch) and programming languages such as Python.
  • Familiarity with modern AI models, APIs, and SDKs (e.g. OpenAI, LLMs)
  • An ability to work full-stack with data; some data engineering experience is a big plus
  • Strong analytical skills and the ability to work with large, complex data sets.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively in a remote work environment across multiple time zones.
  • A passion for blockchain, crypto, and Web3 technologies.
  • Have an AI-first mindset.  At Nansen, AI is more than a tool - it's a mindset.  An ideal candidate is one who enthusiastically embraces AI tools and techniques to streamline processes and elevate outcomes.

What We Offer:

  • Competitive salary and generous equity.
  • Remote work environment with a flexible schedule.
  • A team that values data and AI; our founders are data engineers and data scientists
  • The opportunity to work from our Tech Hubs (flight & accommodation paid for) in Singapore, Bangkok, London & Oslo.
  • A company culture that values transparency, speed, courage, and curiosity.
  • Opportunities for personal and professional growth as the company scales.
  • Exposure to a global network of industry experts, partners, and influencers.
