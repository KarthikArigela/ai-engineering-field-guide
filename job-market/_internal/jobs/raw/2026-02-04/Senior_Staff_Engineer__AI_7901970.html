About Arc

Arc is building the first AI-native business cash management platform. We empower high-growth companies to manage, analyze, and act on their financial data through a unified cash management, treasury, and debt-financing platform—powered by AI.

Founded in 2021, Arc is backed by world-class investors (Series A), supports thousands of technology companies as customers, and manages billions of dollars in transaction volume. With offices in NYC and SF, we operate on a Monday–Wednesday in-office model (Thu–Fri optional in-office or WFH).

We’re at an inflection point.

Large enough to matter; small enough to innovate.

The Role

We are seeking a Senior Staff Engineer, AI to join Arc’s engineering organization as a key technical leader. This is a foundational AI role, responsible for driving the research direction, execution, and delivery of Arc’s AI-powered products across both cash management and debt marketplace products.

What You’ll DoLeadership & Strategy

  • Drive Arc’s most critical AI initiatives end-to-end in a cross-functional environment.

  • Serve as a company-wide thought leader on foundational models, LLMs, agent systems, retrieval, and emerging research.

  • Establish and refine Arc’s internal AI strategy, research direction, and technology roadmap.

Execution & Product Impact

  • Collaborate with engineering, product, design, and leadership to build and improve AI-powered financial products.

  • Build evaluation frameworks, model-improvement infrastructure, and production-grade AI systems.

  • Rapidly prototype 0→1 and MVP experiences to unlock new value for Arc customers.

  • Ship projects frequently and consistently, with strong bias to action.

Technical Excellence

  • Architect and implement Generative AI applications using:

    • Agentic workflows

    • RAG systems

    • State-of-the-art model leveraging techniques

  • Optimize for inference performance, token efficiency, latency, and cost.

  • Contribute to platform-level systems in Python and Typescript.

What You BringMinimum Qualifications

  • 8+ years of engineering experience, including 1+ years building AI-based applications.

  • Advanced degree in Computer Science or related field; Masters/PhD preferred

  • Strong background in Generative AI, LLMs, and modern applied AI toolchains.

  • Demonstrated success building agentic systems, RAG architectures, or state-of-the-art model integrations.

  • Experience building evaluation pipelines for foundational-model applications.

  • Strong software engineering fundamentals.

  • Proven ability to independently drive 0→1, MVP development, and rapid prototyping.

Preferred Experience

  • Startup experience, ideally combined with exposure to 1–2 mid-size or large organizations.

  • Fintech or B2B product experience.

  • Applied AI experience including infra, model improvement loops, and optimization.

  • Python, Typescript, model performance tuning, and workflow automation.

Why Join Arc Now?

  • Category creation: You’ll build the first AI platform for business cash business management.

  • Massive market: Banking + treasury + AI automation is a multi-trillion-dollar opportunity.

  • PMF already secured: Arc has elite customers, strong economics, and hyper-growth.

  • High impact: Staff engineers here are foundational—this role moves the roadmap.

  • Lean, elite team: Work with exceptional builders; every engineer is high-leverage.

Skills Required

  • 8+ years of engineering experience
  • 1+ years building AI-based applications
  • Advanced degree in Computer Science or related field
  • Strong background in Generative AI
  • Demonstrated success building agentic systems or RAG architectures
  • Experience building evaluation pipelines for foundational-model applications
  • Strong software engineering fundamentals
  • Proven ability to independently drive 0 - 1 and MVP development

What We Do

Arc is the capital management platform for ambitious companies and private investors. Arc serves the innovation economy with intelligent cash management and capital markets solutions, in partnership with leading financial institutions and a proprietary network of private credit funds. Founded in 2021, Arc has headquarters in San Francisco and New York City. It has raised over $180 million of equity and debt capital from investors including Left Lane Capital, NFX, Atalaya, Bain Capital Ventures, Clocktower Technology Ventures, Torch Capital, and Y Combinator, among others. To learn more, visit www.joinarc.com. Arc is a financial technology company, not a bank. For important information about Arc, see our general disclosures: https://www.joinarc.com/general-disclosures
