Company Overview: 

Leading with our core values of Quality, Integrity, and Opportunity, MedInsight is one of the healthcare industry’s most trusted solutions for healthcare intelligence. Our company purpose is to empower easy, data-driven decision-making on important healthcare questions. Through our products, education, and services, MedInsight is making an impact on healthcare by helping to drive better outcomes for patients while reducing waste. Over 300 leading healthcare organizations have come to rely on MedInsight analytic solutions for healthcare cost and care management. MedInsight has been ranked #1 for Payer Quality Analytics by clients for the last three years in the Best in KLAS report.  

MedInsight is a subsidiary of Milliman; a global, employee-owned consultancy providing actuarial consulting, retirement funding and healthcare financing, enterprise risk management and regulatory compliance, data analytics and business transformation as well as a range of other consulting and technology solutions. 

Position Summary: 

We’re seeking a Senior AI Engineer with deep expertise in healthcare claims and machine learning to design, build, and deploy production-grade AI systems. You’ll work across platform engineering, data science, product, and clinical teams to create solutions that improve healthcare quality, risk adjustment, and population health insights. This role blends traditional ML, MLOps, and generative AI (Gen AI) to drive impactful analytics products.

What You’ll Work On:

  • MIKE (MedInsight Knowledge Engine): AI-assistant for healthcare analytics 
  • Data Confidence Model (DCM): AI-enhanced data validation and quality assurance 
  • Risk Adjustment AI: HCC recapture, risk scoring automation and predictive modeling
  • Claims Analytics AI: Pattern recognition, fraud detection, and utilization forecasting 
  • Population Health Models: Member segmentation, risk stratification, and predictive analytics 
  • Healthcare NLP: Clinical coding, documentation improvement, and insights extraction 
  • Lakehouse AI Integration: Embedding AI across our healthcare data platform 

Primary Responsibilities:

  • Design, build, and deploy production ML models and MLOps pipelines
  • Engineer feature-rich solutions for healthcare claims, risk prediction, and population health
  • Implement scalable inference systems with proper validation, monitoring, and performance tracking
  • Strategically integrate LLMs and Gen AI into analytics products, building RAG systems and conversational interfaces
  • Collaborate with engineering, product, and clinical teams to translate business needs into AI solutions
  • Ensure models meet clinical accuracy, regulatory compliance, and business credibility
  • Contribute to ML strategy, tooling, and best practices across teams

Requirements:

Machine Learning & AI Expertise

  • 5+ years of ML engineering experience with production deployments
  • Strong Python skills; familiarity with scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, TensorFlow
  • Experience with supervised/unsupervised learning, feature engineering, model evaluation, and MLOps
  • Cloud ML platform experience (Azure ML, Databricks, SageMaker) and containerization (Docker/Kubernetes)
  • Knowledge of generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering, and RAG systems

Healthcare Analytics Experience

  • 3+ years working with healthcare claims data (medical, pharmacy, eligibility)
  • Experience with population health analytics, risk adjustment (CMS-HCC, HHS-HCC), and quality measures
  • Familiarity with actuarial forecasting, cost trending, and healthcare operations

Collaboration & Communication

  • Strong cross-functional collaboration skills, able to work with engineers, product managers, and clinical experts
  • Able to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders
  • Ownership mentality: seeing initiatives from concept to production

Preferred Qualifications

  • Advanced degree (MS/PhD) in Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Math, or related field
  • Experience with healthcare analytics platforms (MedInsight, HealthEdge, Cotiviti)
  • Knowledge of HEDIS, Star Ratings, and quality improvement programs
  • Clinical NLP or medical coding experience
  • HIPAA compliance and healthcare data security knowledge

Technical Environment

  • Languages: Python, SQL, PySpark
  • ML Engineering: scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, TensorFlow, MLflow, Optuna
  • Gen AI: LangChain, OpenAI API, Azure OpenAI, HuggingFace, vector databases
  • Data Platforms: Azure Databricks, Azure Data Lake, PostgreSQL, Delta Lake
  • Cloud & MLOps: Azure ML, Azure DevOps, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines
  • Analytics: PowerBI, Jupyter, VS Code, Git

What you need to know about the Los Angeles Tech Scene

Los Angeles is a global leader in entertainment, so it’s no surprise that many of the biggest players in streaming, digital media and game development call the city home. But the city boasts plenty of non-entertainment innovation as well, with tech companies spanning verticals like AI, fintech, e-commerce and biotech. With major universities like Caltech, UCLA, USC and the nearby UC Irvine, the city has a steady supply of top-flight tech and engineering talent — not counting the graduates flocking to Los Angeles from across the world to enjoy its beaches, culture and year-round temperate climate.

Key Facts About Los Angeles Tech

  • Number of Tech Workers: 375,800; 5.5% of overall workforce (2024 CompTIA survey)
  • Major Tech Employers: Snap, Netflix, SpaceX, Disney, Google
  • Key Industries: Artificial intelligence, adtech, media, software, game development
  • Funding Landscape: $11.6 billion in venture capital funding in 2024 (Pitchbook)
  • Notable Investors: Strong Ventures, Fifth Wall, Upfront Ventures, Mucker Capital, Kittyhawk Ventures
  • Research Centers and Universities: California Institute of Technology, UCLA, University of Southern California, UC Irvine, Pepperdine, California Institute for Immunology and Immunotherapy, Center for Quantum Science and Engineering
