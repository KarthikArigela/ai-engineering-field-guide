About FREENOW

FREENOW empowers smarter mobility decisions, helping people move freely and cities thrive. As Europe’s leading Mobility Super App, we serve more than 54 million users across 16 markets and 170+ cities.

AI is becoming core to how we build products, operate the platform, and support our teams. To move from experimentation to real, measurable impact, we are building a central AI Enablement & Evaluation team — and we’re looking for a Senior AI Engineer to help turn AI into a reliable, scalable capability across the company.

If you enjoy building AI systems that actually ship, scale, and deliver value — not just demos — this is your next ride 🚕

Your Mission

As a Senior AI Engineer, you will sit at the intersection of AI engineering, evaluation, and enablement. Your role is to ensure that AI systems at FREENOW are production-ready, measurable, and safe, while accelerating adoption across product and engineering teams.

You will help transform AI from isolated pilots into reusable platforms, tools, and workflows used across the organization.

Your Daily Adventures Will Include
  • Designing, building, and maintaining production-grade AI systems using LLMs, agents, and retrieval-based architectures
  • Owning and evolving the AI Evaluation Framework, including:
    • multi-turn evaluations
    • quality, safety, and hallucination metrics
    • offline and production-data evaluation
  • Partnering with Product, Engineering, Legal, and Security to ensure AI solutions are compliant, observable, and trustworthy
  • Supporting and scaling internal AI products such as FreenowGPT and domain-specific AI assistants
  • Enabling teams through shared tooling, patterns, and best practices (e.g. Gemini enablement, prompt & agent patterns)
  • Building reusable AI components (RAG pipelines, agent orchestration, model access layers)
  • Helping define standards for agent-ready architecture (MCP integrations, model abstraction, permissions)
  • Monitoring AI system performance, cost, and reliability in production
  • Acting as a technical reference point for AI across squads — from experimentation to rollout
Our AI & Tech Stack

Python | LLM APIs (Gemini, OpenAI) | Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Databricks | Cloud Infrastructure | APIs & Backend Services
Evaluation tooling | Observability & monitoring | Internal developer tooling

(Exact tools matter less than strong engineering fundamentals — the stack will evolve.)

To Be Successful in This Role
  • You have strong experience as an AI Engineer, ML Engineer, or Senior Software Engineer working with modern AI systems
  • You’ve built or operated LLM-based systems in production, not just prototypes
  • You understand the failure modes of LLMs (hallucinations, non-determinism, evaluation challenges)
  • You consistently balance speed, quality, and pragmatism; knowing when to ship, when to iterate, and when correctness is non-negotiable in production AI systems.
  • You are comfortable designing evaluation frameworks and defining quality metrics for AI behavior
  • You have solid backend engineering fundamentals (APIs, services, data access)
  • You enjoy working across teams and explaining complex AI concepts clearly
  • You care about reliability, safety, and real business impact, not hype
  • You are fluent in English
Nice to Have
  • Experience with agentic systems or multi-step workflows
  • Familiarity with AI governance, risk, or compliance considerations (e.g. GDPR, EU AI Act)
  • Experience enabling other teams through platforms, SDKs, or internal tools
  • Prior work in marketplaces, mobility, or large-scale consumer products
Why This Role Matters

Most companies experiment with AI. Very few manage to scale it responsibly and profitably.

This role is central to:

  • reducing AI risk in customer-facing systems (support, dispatch, booking),
  • unlocking company-wide productivity through internal AI products,
  • and building the technical foundations that allow FREENOW to move fast without breaking trust.

You’ll help define how AI is built at FREENOW for years to come.

What We Do

FREENOW is the Mobility Super App with the largest vehicle choice for consumers across Europe available in 9 markets and in over 150 cities. Users can access all types of mobility services within a single app including taxis, Ride (PHV), Carsharing, eScooters, eBikes and eMopeds and public transport. FREENOW aggregates numerous mobility brands with the ambition to make urban mobility more efficient and sustainable without adding new vehicles on the street. The Germany-based company is backed by the BMW Group and Mercedes-Benz Mobility. In total, around 1,000 employees in more than 26 offices work for the services of FREENOW, which is led by CEO Thomas Zimmermann.
