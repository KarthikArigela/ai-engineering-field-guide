Technical Lead - AI Specialist / Automation Engineer

The Role
Design, build, and deploy custom AI-driven tools, agents, and automated pipelines using LLMs and vector databases. Partner with Dev, Creative, and QA to automate art/animation asset generation, code/documentation workflows, and QA test generation. Implement integrations with GitHub, Jira, CMS, Figma, Adobe, and communication platforms; optimize prompts, fine-tune models, monitor performance, and ensure data privacy and cost efficiency.
Summary Generated by Built In

At Aristocrat, we're on a mission to bring happiness to life through the power of play. We believe our innovative and inclusive culture drives our success. We're committed to making a positive impact in the gaming industry. As a Technical Lead - AI Specialist / Automation Engineer, you'll have the chance to develop the future of our AI-based tools and platforms. You will make a significant impact within our engineering, creative, and quality assurance teams. This is an outstanding opportunity for someone passionate about using modern technologies to improve our workflows and deliver world-class gaming experiences. Join our ambitious team and help us continue to raise the standard in responsible gameplay and sustainability!

What You'll Do
  • Cross-Functional AI Tooling: Partner with Dev, Creative, and QA leads to identify operational bottlenecks and engineer tailored AI solutions.
  • Build and prioritize custom graphics tools to capture and multiply both static and animated art assets. These tools ensure they closely match our studio's outstanding art style.
  • Architecture & Development: Build, test, and deploy internal AI applications, custom web portals, and Slack/Teams bots using modern LLM APIs.
  • Workflow Automation: Build end-to-end automated pipelines connecting software systems like Jira, GitHub, and CMS using orchestration frameworks.
  • Prompt Engineering & Fine-Tuning: Develop, evaluate, and optimize complex system prompts and workflows for high-quality outputs.
  • Evaluation & Maintenance: Monitor tool performance, manage API cost optimization, mitigate model hallucination, and ensure strict data privacy and security compliance.
Domain-Specific Impact Areas
  • For Dev & Math Teams: Automate tasks such as summarizing technical documentation, generating boilerplate code, and AI-assisted code reviews. Develop specialized AI tools to assist with complex math tooling and game balancing.
  • For Creative Teams: Build asset-generation pipelines, automated game rules generation, and tools to generate themes that help Artists/Animators scale content securely.
  • For QA Teams: Build agents that parse acceptance criteria to generate comprehensive test cases, automate synthetic test data generation, generate submission documents, and categorize bug reports.
What We're Looking For
  • Experience: 5+ years in software engineering, with at least 1.5+ years of hands-on experience building production-grade AI/LLM applications.
  • Specialized Expertise: Strong experience with art and animation pipelines is highly favorable.
  • Programming: Proficiency in Python or TypeScript/Node.js.
  • AI Ecosystem: Deep familiarity with LLM APIs, orchestration frameworks, and Vector Databases.
  • Integrations: Proven track record of working with REST APIs, webhooks, and SDKs for various platforms like GitHub, Jira, Figma, Adobe, Slack, and Teams.
Soft Skills & Approach
  • Product Approach: Ability to interview internal users, translate their difficulties into technical requirements, and build intuitive UX/UI.
  • Ambiguity Champion: Comfortable working in a rapidly shifting AI landscape, evaluating new frameworks, and picking the right tool for the job.
Qualification
  • Education: Computer Science Education (B.Tech/BE/MCA).

  • Mastery of Python or TypeScript/Node.js
  • Deep familiarity with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic/Claude), open-source models, orchestration frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, LangGraph), and vector databases
  • Experience integrating with REST APIs, webhooks, and SDKs and working with GitHub, Jira, Figma, Adobe, Slack, and Microsoft Teams
