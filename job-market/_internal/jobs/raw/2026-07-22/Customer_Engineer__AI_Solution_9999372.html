What we're looking for: 

We are looking for a highly technical, customer-focused Customer Engineer, AI Solutions to help scale one of Nitra's fastest-growing businesses. This role sits at the intersection of Sales, Customer Success, Product, and Engineering and will be responsible for helping healthcare practices successfully evaluate, implement, adopt, and expand Nitra's AI software platform.

This is a highly cross-functional role with three core responsibilities. First, you'll partner with Sales and Customer Success to secure the technical win for both new customer acquisitions and cross-sell opportunities. Second, you'll own successful customer implementations, ensuring customers quickly realize value from our AI platform. Third, you'll serve as the voice of the customer by working closely with Product, Product Marketing, and Engineering to influence roadmap decisions and continuously improve the customer experience.

The ideal candidate combines strong technical aptitude with exceptional customer-facing skills. You enjoy solving complex customer problems, translating technical capabilities into business value, and building repeatable implementation processes that scale. If you're excited about AI, healthcare technology, and working in a fast-paced startup environment, we'd love to meet you.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Partner with Sales and Customer Success throughout the sales cycle by leading technical discovery, solution design, product demonstrations, proof-of-concepts, technical evaluations, and implementation planning.
  • Serve as the technical expert during customer engagements, helping healthcare organizations understand how Nitra's AI platform can automate workflows and improve operational efficiency.
  • Lead customer implementations from kickoff through production launch, including solution configuration, integrations, testing, training, validation, and go-live support.
  • Drive customer adoption by helping practices implement best practices, optimize workflows, and maximize long-term value from Nitra's AI software.
  • Partner closely with Product, Product Marketing, and Engineering to prioritize customer feedback, influence product roadmap decisions, and improve the customer experience.
  • Build scalable implementation methodologies, technical documentation, playbooks, demo environments, onboarding guides, and best practices that improve deployment quality and reduce time-to-value.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and collaborate with Engineering to resolve complex customer challenges.
  • Measure and optimize implementation performance, technical win rates, customer adoption, customer satisfaction, and implementation quality.
  • Act as a trusted technical advisor throughout the customer lifecycle while identifying opportunities for expansion and additional product adoption.

  • Experience leading SaaS or enterprise software implementations from technical discovery through production launch.

What We Do

Nitra’s mission is to overhaul the healthcare industry with radically efficient and transparent solutions. Nitra empowers providers to find the right balance between supporting their patients and running their practice – starting with tools that deliver a simpler, smarter way to manage their spending. Nitra solutions integrate seamlessly with the complex business processes of the industry. Our goal is to provide an ecosystem that helps doctors better manage their practices, so they can focus on their patients. Starting with the first business card built specifically for doctors, Nitra will expand to bring physicians and medical clinics around the country the loans, accounts, payments, and expense management products they expect, in an all-in-one platform powered by machine learning and blockchain technologies. The company was created by unicorn founders who have successfully scaled to thousands of customers. They are joined by an ambitious and experienced team from Amazon, American Express, Citi, Dropbox, Facebook, Mastercard and PayPal. The team is supported by an expert group of Advisors such as the cofounders of Square and Xendit, executives from Intuit, Governors and White House senior staffers, and is backed by some of the world’s leading VCs such as Andreessen Horowitz and NEA.
