About Commvault 

Commvault (NASDAQ: CVLT) is the gold standard in cyber resilience. The company empowers customers to uncover, take action, and rapidly recover from cyberattacks – keeping data safe and businesses resilient. The company’s unique AI-powered platform combines best-in-class data protection, exceptional data security, advanced data intelligence, and lightning-fast recovery across any workload or cloud at the lowest TCO. Senior Engineer- AI & Automation

The Opportunity

We have an outstanding career opportunity for Senior Engineer- AI & Automation at Bangalore, Hyderabad and Pune locations. We’re seeking a Senior AI/ML & Automation Engineer for our Solutions Engineering team to deliver AI-driven solutions and automated workflows using Commvault APIs, automation tools, and cloud services. You’ll work independently or collaboratively in a distributed team, balancing innovation with strong execution, documentation, and a customer-first mindset.

What you’ll do…

  • Build and deploy AI/ML solutions (NLP, computer vision, supervised/unsupervised) informed by data analysis.
  • Partner with product and customer teams to embed AI into workflows that meet objectives and compliance needs.
  • Run discovery with technical/non-technical stakeholders; translate requirements into scoped designs and outcomes.
  • Deliver and document solutions using Commvault APIs, built-in automation/reporting, and third-party integrations.
  • Implement automation and reporting with PowerShell/shell/orchestration; use AI coding tools while upholding standards.
  • Support remote delivery: deploy, test, and hand over solutions to our customers.
  • Manage time across projects; lead customer discussions, capture notes, and drive action items.

Who you are…

  • Bachelor's degree required.
  • 6+ years in IT with strong enterprise infrastructure, application operations, and process expertise.
  • 4+ years in project-based delivery teams (customer-facing or cross-functional).
  • 3+ years delivering automation and reporting (scripting/orchestration).
  • 2+ years programming (Python preferred) with ML frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch/Scikit-learn).
  • Cloud experience (AWS/Azure/GCP), including AI services.
  • Comfortable using AI-assisted coding tools while maintaining quality via reviews and standards.
  • Strong OS, storage, networking, security, and cloud fundamentals.
  • SQL Server (T-SQL) query skills for reporting/automation.
  • Scripting with Java/Groovy/JavaScript (or another OO language).
  • REST API integration experience (tokens, OAuth, basic/cert auth).
  • CI/CD and version control knowledge; hands-on with GitLab CI and/or Azure DevOps.
  • Commvault experience (preferred) or another enterprise backup/recovery platform.
  • Proven use of AI-assisted tools for development, troubleshooting, documentation, and solution design.
  • Orchestration in private clouds (VMware/Hyper-V) and public clouds (AWS/Azure).
  • Security and SaaS experience strongly preferred.

You’ll love working here because:

  • Employee stock purchase plan (ESPP)
  • Continuous professional development, product training, and career pathing
  • Annual health check-ups, Car lease Program, and Tuition Reimbursement
  • An inclusive company culture, an opportunity to join our Community Guilds
  • Personal accident cover and Term life cover
