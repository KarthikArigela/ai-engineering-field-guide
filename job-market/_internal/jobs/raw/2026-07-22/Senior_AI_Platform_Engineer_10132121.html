The role ⚡

📍 London (Hybrid) | 🤝 2 days in the office | 💰 Competitive Salary + Benefits

Data is core to our mission at Funding Circle to build a better financial world as well as being our key asset that everyone across the organisation has a part to play in making sure that we look after it, allowing us to better support our customers.

Our central data team lives in our Tech org and reports to the VP of Data. In order to strengthen our data maturity and become truly data driven we are committed to:

  • Building a strong data culture where data is treated as an asset

  • Empowering our circlers to become highly data fluent and confident self serve users

  • Enhancing our world class data platform

  • Creating data products across all domains

  • Transforming our business with ML & AI

You will be joining our AI and Machine Learning team, taking a pivotal role in building the platform that underpins Funding Circle's AI strategy and allows our existing GenAI applications to scale. This is a senior, hands-on technical role focused on enabling the development, deployment, and management of AI applications and agents across the business.

  • Architect, build, maintain cloud-based infrastructure for hosting and serving AI (ML and GenAI) models and applications.

  • Develop and design tooling and frameworks to standardise AI development, implement systems for central discovery, monitoring, and governance of AI agents, and contribute to the technical evaluation and integration of third-party AI tools.

  • Work closely with stakeholders (Product, Data Science, Engineering, Security, Legal) to ensure the platform meets business needs and embed Funding Circle's Responsible AI framework into the platform's design and operation.

  • Act as a subject matter expert on AI platforms and infrastructure, establish best practices, share knowledge, potentially mentor other engineers, and stay current with the latest AI/ML technologies.

What we’re looking for 🌱

Experience as an engineer within the AI space is preferred. However this role is also suitable for Senior and Lead engineers with experience and skills in product engineering, data engineer, cloud platform engineering who are looking to deepen their AI expertise.

  • Understanding and hands-on experience with LLM based applications and related technologies (e.g. RAG, vector databases, agent frameworks). Experience managing and deploying AI Agent and Knowledge Management systems is a plus (e.g Glean, Gemini Enterprise, Amazon Quick, Writer, Sana etc.)

  • Proven ability to design, build, and operate scalable, secure systems on cloud platforms, using Infrastructure as Code, containerisation, serverless, data storage

  • Excellent ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams, manage stakeholders, and clearly explain complex technical ideas

  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills with a practical, results-oriented approach, the ability to navigate ambiguity, take ownership, and drive initiatives forward autonomously

  • Experience in the design and implementation of full-stack applications and strong programming skills is a strong advantage

We’re building a place where everyone truly feels they belong. Even if your past experience doesn't align perfectly with every requirement, we'd still love to hear from you.

Skills Required

  • Hands-on experience with LLM-based applications and related technologies (RAG, vector databases, agent frameworks)
  • Experience managing or deploying AI agent and knowledge management systems (examples listed as a plus)
  • Proven ability to design, build, and operate scalable, secure cloud systems using Infrastructure as Code, containerisation, serverless, and data storage

  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, ability to take ownership and work autonomously in ambiguous situations

What We Do

Over the past ten years, Funding Circle has built a machine learning and technology platform that is changing the way small businesses access the financing they need to grow.
