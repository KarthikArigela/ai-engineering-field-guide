The Dataset

6,964

job postings from 2,499 companies

Searched "AI Engineer" on builtin.com - LA, NY, London, Amsterdam, Berlin, India - 8 monthly scrapes, Feb 4 to Aug 25, 2026

Why Job Postings, Not Opinions

  • Every hot take about "what AI engineers do" is somebody's opinion
  • Job postings are what 2,499 employers are actually willing to pay for, this month
  • Zero job-ID overlap between scrapes - each month is an independent cross-section of the market


This talk is the data. role/02-skills.md has the full writeup.

The key question for any posting: does this role work ON AI systems, or NEAR them?

RAG + Agents Are the Baseline

  • LLMs - 62.2% of all jobs
  • Agents (any framing) - 55.4%
  • RAG - 39.8% - the single most common named pattern in the dataset
  • Prompt engineering - 34.7%


Within AI-First roles specifically: RAG 53.9%, agents 71.6%. Learn these two patterns deeply and you cover most of the work.

27.3% of AI-First roles mention fine-tuning somewhere - but it's the main job for only 3.6%. Focus on RAG and agents first; learn fine-tuning for domain-specific work (healthcare, legal, finance).

Key Takeaways

  • RAG and agents are what employers actually pay for - fine-tuning and PyTorch-style training are niche by comparison
  • Frameworks travel together - LangChain, LangGraph, and LlamaIndex show up as an ecosystem, not competing choices
  • Fine-tuning is mentioned often but rarely the main job - only 3.6% of AI-First roles focus on it
  • GenAI skills split cleanly between AI-First and AI-Support roles; infrastructure skills don't
