The Extractor Silently Changed

The LLM extraction model switched glm-5.1 to glm-5.2 partway through the six original scrapes


Some of what I previously reported as "market trends" was actually the extractor drifting, not the market moving

Two "Trends" That Were Actually Noise

  • SQL was reported as the fastest-rising skill (+3.59 pp/scrape). On corrected data: a modest +0.5 pp/scrape.
  • ai-first share was reported climbing 69.4% → 75.9%. On corrected data: flat, 68-71% the whole time.


Everything from here on uses the corrected, consistent extraction

What Still Hasn't Happened

Key Takeaways

  • ai-first share is stable (68-71%), not rising - earlier "consolidation" was measurement noise
  • Integrator-vs-trainer shift is real and accelerating: 80% → 88% integrator within ai-first
  • Prompt Engineering and RAG are the fastest-rising named skills, not SQL
  • Agent-building is fragmenting into more specific sub-roles, not consolidating
  • AI infra still isn't a standalone role; junior hiring is still closed
